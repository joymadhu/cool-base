Base Cool 101

01

What is Base?

An Ethereum Layer 2 built on the OP Stack and incubated by Coinbase. Transactions settle to Ethereum at a fraction of the cost.

02

Blocks

Base produces a new block about every 2 seconds. Each block bundles transactions and is later posted to Ethereum.

03

Volume

DEX volume is the total USD value swapped on decentralized exchanges on Base. The all-time high is the busiest single day ever.
